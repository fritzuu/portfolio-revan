Kenney attribution is voluntary; credit included here. Assets are served locally, never hotlinked. Custom animation poses remain necessary for the visitor's configurable skin/outfit and recognizable Messi/Yamal cameos when researched packs do not fit.

Garden scenery and pergola gate are original procedural pixel art in `src/game/gardenArt.js`; garden trees reuse the original town renderer. No downloaded tiles are used in the garden or gate.
